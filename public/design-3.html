FAQs

Common Questions

Find quick answers to the most frequently asked questions regarding our services.

What advertising services do you offer?
We offer a comprehensive range of services including Offline Media, Transit Media, Electronic Media, Cinema Branding, Ambient Media, Print Media, Non-Traditional Media, Corporate Gifting, and Sports & Showbiz advertising. Each service is tailored to meet your specific brand objectives.
Which cities do you operate in?
We have a strong presence across India with a particular focus on Pune, Mumbai, Delhi, and other major metropolitan cities. Our railway station branding covers key stations like Pune, Pimpri, Chinchwad, Vadgaon, Lonavala, and many more across the country.
What is the minimum budget for an advertising campaign?
Our advertising solutions are highly scalable. We work with brands of all sizes, from small local businesses to large corporate entities. Contact us directly for a customized quote based on your specific requirements and overall campaign objectives.
How do I get started with Pipani Advertising?
Simply reach out through our contact form below or call our co-founders directly. We'll schedule an initial consultation to understand your needs and propose the best advertising strategy for your brand. You can also email us at yasin@pipaniadvertising.com.
